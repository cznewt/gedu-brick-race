# Licence of the models

The models in this folder and everything the builder makes from them are licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
That covers the scripts, their JSON and the `.ldr` files, pictures, booklets and parts lists in a release.

The custom part files in a model's Studio zip (`parts/*.dat`, written by `builder/customparts.py`)
are [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), as their headers say. They are the
same parts the GeekEdu site gives out, and CC BY 4.0 is what LDraw parts use.

Attribution: GeekEdu, https://github.com/cznewt/gedu-lego-racing

- `wukong-mecanum` reproduces the mecanum car of the ElecFreaks Wonder Rugged Kit from its booklet.
  The design is ElecFreaks'.
- `wukong-tank-2-motors`, `wukong-tank-4-motors` and `wukong-tank-4-motors-geared` are GeekEdu
  designs on that car's body, with tracks from the Yahboom Building:bit kit.
