# Compact mecanum car

A users' model by Aleš Komárek (2026-10-31). Four Geekservo 360 servos stand at the corners on one lug
line, outputs outwards, the 60 mm mecanum wheels straight on them. The frame has four layers: at the
bottom a yellow 9-beam a side on the inner lugs; at the servos' height a white 5-beam a side between
the servos and a lime 7-beam across each end beside the outer lugs; on top, along each side, the
kit's blue 1 x 4 beam with an axle hole, a lime 5-beam, the kit's yellow 1 x 4 and a yellow 5-beam;
and across, on top of those, a white 7-beam at the front, a yellow 7-beam over the front inner lugs
and the yellow 7-beam of the back U. The Wukong board stands across the middle on the side beams,
the micro:bit lying flat towards the front, a grey 7-beam screwed up its right edge. It drives with
the Brick Race racer's driver (the *Elecfreak Wukong Mecanum Car* firmware, no gears, so DIRECTION
stays 1).

had the car upside down): its booklet, Studio file and parts list come with the next release. Two
parts are the Wonder Building Kit's 1 x 4 beams (not LEGO's), drawn as custom parts. Still from
the photos alone: which way the blue pins in the end beams point, and the board's mount (the grey
7-beam is drawn screwed to the board, without a pin).
