# Compact mecanum car

A users' model by Aleš Komárek (2026-10-31). Four Geekservo 360 servos stand at the corners, their inner
lugs six studs apart, outputs outwards, the 60 mm mecanum wheels straight on them. The frame has four
layers: at the bottom a yellow 9-beam a side pinned up into the inner lugs; at the servos' upper half a
white 5-beam a side between the inner lugs and a lime 7-beam across each end (two studs before the front
lugs, one behind the back ones), their blue pins pointing up; on top, along each side, the kit's blue L
piece, a lime 5-beam, the kit's yellow 1 x 4 and a yellow 5-beam; and across, on top of those, a white
7-beam at the front on the L pieces, a yellow 7-beam over the front inner lugs and the yellow 7-beam of
the back U, straight over the back end beam. The Wukong board stands across the middle on the side
beams, pinned down through its base's edge holes, the micro:bit lying flat towards the front, a grey
3 x 5 L-liftarm screwed to its right corner. It drives with the Brick Race racer's driver (the
*Elecfreak Wukong Mecanum Car* firmware, no gears, so DIRECTION stays 1).
